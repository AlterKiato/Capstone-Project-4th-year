Prepared: 2026-11-12. **DRAFT — NOT APPROVED, SIGNED OR ACTIVE.**

This is a proposed narrow addendum to institutional AI authorization for ThesiSHS AI. The scope of previously reported signed AI documentation remains **UNVERIFIED** without its text. This proposal supplies no retrospective approval and changes no existing acceptance record, eligibility decision or application setting.

## Decision requested before a fictional-text pilot

Permit a controlled evaluation of live Groq-generated research assistance in the existing Adviser Research Assistant, using **only the bundled fictional excerpt** in `lib/ai/adviser-research-demo.ts` and reviewed predefined questions. The three existing tasks are:

- Academic Writing Review (`writing`).
- Research Consistency Review (`consistency`).
- Research Methodology Guidance (`methodology`).

Participants must be individually authorized adults aged 18+, with active Adviser accounts and current Main or exact open Co-Adviser review access. Main designation or Co assignment alone establishes neither adulthood nor pilot permission. Students, Panel users and Admin aggregate interpretation are outside this pilot.

The requested exception to the current aggregate-only provider boundary covers this fictional fixture alone. It excludes arbitrary pasted text, actual student manuscripts, real titles, abstracts, PDFs, official feedback, recommendations, identifiers and signed URLs. No automatic extraction, stored transcripts, academic writes, grading or approval/rejection decisions are permitted. Only the approved fixture, fixed task instructions and reviewed question may enter the provider payload; account/resource identifiers remain internal.

## Provider disclosure and conditions

Groq is the proposed external provider. Its documentation describes default non-retention of inference content, with reliability/abuse exceptions of up to 30 days or longer if legally required. ZDR disables that content retention; usage metadata remains collected. Retained customer content is stored in US GCP buckets. **Actual organization settings are NOT VERIFIED.** Require verified ZDR for this pilot; do not promise absolute zero retention or local residency. [Groq data controls](https://console.groq.com/docs/your-data)

An authorized institutional representative must review the applicable agreement, supplemental/model terms and privacy obligations. No training permission, paid upgrade, alternate provider or scope expansion is proposed. [Services Agreement](https://console.groq.com/docs/legal/services-agreement)

Proposed limits for review: 3 attempts/account/UTC day, 10 globally/day, 120-second cooldown, one concurrent request, and at most 2 requests in an approved window of no more than 2 hours. These are proposed ceilings, not verified live controls. Final token limits and provider plan/allowances must be confirmed before activation.
