## Proposed participant notice

**Purpose and choice.** ThesiSHS AI proposes an optional live Groq pilot for Academic Writing Review, Research Consistency Review and Research Methodology Guidance. Participation is limited to independently verified adults aged 18+ who are individually authorized Main/assigned Co-Advisers. Declining, closing the notice or withdrawing will not remove normal account, research, review or static-guidance access.

**Material sent.** Groq would receive only the bundled fictional excerpt, approved task instructions and a reviewed predefined question. Do not submit real manuscripts, titles, abstracts, PDFs, feedback, private recommendations, identities or other pasted text. The pilot will not extract manuscript content. Account, submission and assignment identifiers stay within the application authorization layer.

**External processing.** Groq documents default non-retention of inference content, with reliability/abuse logging exceptions of up to 30 days or longer when legally required. The pilot requires verified ZDR, which disables that content retention; usage metadata is still collected. Retained customer content is stored in US GCP buckets. This is not an absolute zero-retention promise. No permission to train on pilot inputs/outputs is granted. [Data controls](https://console.groq.com/docs/your-data), [Services Agreement](https://console.groq.com/docs/legal/services-agreement)

**Limitations and records.** Responses would be generated by Groq and may be inaccurate, incomplete or misleading. They concern fictional material only and require human review. They do not grade research, decide approval/rejection or create official Adviser feedback. There are no academic writes or automatic decisions. The proposed pilot stores no conversation transcript; displayed exchanges remain temporary. Eligibility/consent history and minimal operational quota records are separate from transcripts.
